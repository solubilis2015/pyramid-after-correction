General Civil Works

Cable trenches, duct banks, equipment plinths and plant roadworks supporting electrical and mechanical scopes.

Overview

General Civil Works at Pyramid

Most of our civil work exists to make an electrical or mechanical scope possible: the trench the cable runs in, the plinth the panel stands on, the road crossing that lets a feeder reach the far side of a plant. Keeping that work in the same hands removes the interface that usually delays it.

Where this scope is applied

  • Cable route civil works for electrical packages
  • Foundations for new panels, skids and packages
  • Plant road and drainage maintenance
  • Reinstatement after underground cable installation
  • Small capital project civil scopes

Do you work in live, operating plant?

Yes. The majority of our General Civil Works work is executed inside running facilities under client permit-to-work, isolation and hazardous-area procedures. We hold bizSAFE STAR and are certified to ISO 45001:2018.

Can you supply materials as well as install?

We do, and clients frequently ask us to, so that supply and installation sit with a single accountable party rather than being split across two purchase orders.

Will we get documentation at handover?

Test records, calibration or inspection certificates, as-built marking and punch-list close-out are produced as the work proceeds and issued as a handover pack.
