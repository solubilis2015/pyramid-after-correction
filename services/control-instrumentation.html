Control & Instrumentation

Field instruments, control panels, calibration and loop checking through to commissioning and DCS interface.

Overview

Control & Instrumentation at Pyramid

Instrumentation is where a process plant's control philosophy meets physical reality. Pyramid Engineering designs, installs, calibrates and proves instrument and control systems — from a single replacement transmitter during a running shift to complete C&I packages on new units — and has done so for Chevron, Shell, ExxonMobil, Evonik, DuPont and REC Solar sites under main contractors including Kurihara Kogyo, KPW Engineering, Siemens and Honeywell.

Where this scope is applied

  • Greenfield C&I packages on new process units
  • Turnaround instrument overhaul, calibration and loop re-checks
  • Obsolescence-driven instrument and control system replacement
  • Analyser and sampling system installation
  • Long-term instrumentation maintenance under call-out contract

Standards & codes

  • ISA 5.1 / ISA 20 — instrumentation symbols and specification forms
  • IEC 61511 — functional safety for the process industry
  • IEC 60079 — installation in explosive atmospheres
  • Client loop folder, calibration and punch-list procedures

Scope of work

What we deliver

Instrument installation

  • Field transmitters, gauges, switches, thermowells and analysers
  • Field junction boxes, marshalling and interface cabinets
  • Impulse and pneumatic tubing, manifolds and hook-ups
  • Control valve, actuator and positioner installation
  • Safeguarding and interlock device installation

Cabling and termination

  • Instrument and control cable laying, glanding and termination
  • Segregated routing for intrinsically safe circuits
  • Screen and earthing integrity verification
  • Cable schedules and termination records

Calibration and loop checking

  • Bench and field calibration of all instrument types
  • Full loop checks — field device to DCS/PLC graphic
  • Control valve stroking, positioner calibration and travel checks
  • Calibration certificates and punch-list close-out

Panels, systems and commissioning

  • Design, engineering and build of instrument and control panels
  • PLC / DCS / SCADA interfacing, I/O checks and logic verification
  • Pre-commissioning, commissioning and start-up support
  • Troubleshooting and instrumentation maintenance contracts
  • Supply of instruments, sensors, valves and spares

Do you work in live, operating plant?

Yes. The majority of our Control & Instrumentation work is executed inside running facilities under client permit-to-work, isolation and hazardous-area procedures. We hold bizSAFE STAR and are certified to ISO 45001:2018.

Can you supply materials as well as install?

We do, and clients frequently ask us to, so that supply and installation sit with a single accountable party rather than being split across two purchase orders.

Will we get documentation at handover?

Test records, calibration or inspection certificates, as-built marking and punch-list close-out are produced as the work proceeds and issued as a handover pack.
