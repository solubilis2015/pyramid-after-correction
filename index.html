Who we are

An engineering contractor built around plant that cannot stop

Pyramid Engineering Private Limited was founded in 2008 by a small, dedicated team focused on the Oil & Gas, refinery, petrochemical and power generation industries. We specialise in plant maintenance, small capital projects, shutdown maintenance and start-up support, with deep expertise in electrical and instrumentation works and thermal insulation.

Most of what we do happens inside operating plant — under permit, around live systems, and to a shutdown window that does not move. That constraint shapes how we plan, staff and document every job.

  • Certified to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018, and assessed at bizSAFE STAR
  • Corporate member of ASPRI and the Singapore Welding Society
  • Long-term maintenance relationships measured in years, not job numbers

How we work

The six-stage delivery process

Designed around live plant, fixed shutdown windows and client permit systems.

Enquiry and scope review

We review your drawings, specifications and site constraints and come back with clarifications before pricing, not after.

Proposal and method

A costed proposal with method statement, manpower plan, programme and the standards the work will be executed to.

Mobilisation and permits

Risk assessments, permits, vendor pre-qualification and toolbox briefings completed before the first tool is lifted.

Execution and QA/QC

Multi-discipline execution under supervision, with inspection and test records assembled as the work proceeds.

Commissioning and handover

Loop checks, functional tests, energisation support and punch-list close-out with as-built documentation.

Maintenance and support

Call-out agreements, planned maintenance and shutdown support to keep the asset running after handover.
