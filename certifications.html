Certifications & Compliance

Management systems

Certified, current and independently audited

Our quality and occupational health and safety management systems are certified to a scope written specifically for our work: process and industrial plant consultancy, engineering, construction and maintenance works for chemical, refineries and petrochemical industries.

ISO 9001:2015 — Quality Management

Certificate No. 24EQMV39. Initially registered 26 February 2024, issued 26 February 2024, valid to 25 February 2027. Scope: process and industrial plant consultancy, engineering, construction and maintenance works for chemical, refineries and petrochemical industries. Accredited under EGAC / IAF.

ISO 45001:2018 — Occupational Health & Safety

Certificate No. 24EOMV46. Initially registered 27 February 2024, issued 27 February 2024, valid to 26 February 2027. Same certified scope as our quality management system. Accredited under EGAC / IAF.

bizSAFE STAR — WSH Council

The highest tier of Singapore's bizSAFE programme, awarded by the Workplace Safety and Health Council. Issued 14 March 2024 and valid to 26 February 2027, confirming a workplace safety and health management system assessed against national requirements.

ASPRI Corporate Membership

Corporate member of the Association of Process Industry (formerly the Process Industry Contractors' Association Singapore), membership number C0656/2011, admitted 1 June 2011 — the recognised industry body for process plant contractors on Jurong Island and beyond.

Singapore Welding Society — Corporate Member

Corporate member of the Singapore Welding Society since 1 June 2011, supporting welding competence, procedure qualification and fabrication quality across our mechanical and piping scopes.

Safety in practice

What certification means on your site

A certificate on a wall is not a safety record. These are the practices the certificates describe, applied on every job.

  • Risk assessment and method statement completed and approved before mobilisation
  • Work executed under the client's permit-to-work and isolation procedures
  • Toolbox briefings at the start of every shift, in the languages the crew speaks
  • Hazardous-area and confined-space requirements applied as a matter of routine
  • Equipment issued to site checked, certified and maintained in safe working condition
  • Incident reporting and corrective action tracked through the management system
