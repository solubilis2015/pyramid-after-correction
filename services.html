Capability overview

One contractor, seven disciplines

Most plant projects fail at the interfaces — the day the cable crew waits on the civil crew, or the insulation contractor cannot start because the piping punch list is open. Pyramid Engineering holds those disciplines in-house, which is why clients ask us to take the whole package.

HT and LT cabling, switchgear and power distribution installed, tested and commissioned to Singapore standards.

  • High-voltage and low-voltage cable laying, glanding and termination
  • Cable ladder, tray and trunking fabrication and installation
  • Supply and installation of HT and LT switchgear
  • Power distribution panels, MCCs, distribution boards and isolators
Electrical Engineering in detail

Field instruments, control panels, calibration and loop checking through to commissioning and DCS interface.

  • Field transmitters, gauges, switches, thermowells and analysers
  • Field junction boxes, marshalling and interface cabinets
  • Instrument and control cable laying, glanding and termination
  • Segregated routing for intrinsically safe circuits
Control & Instrumentation in detail

Fire and gas detection, alarm panels, CCTV and access control — supplied, installed and commissioned.

  • Flame, smoke, heat and combustible gas detector installation
  • Manual call points, sounders and beacon installation
  • Fire and gas alarm panel supply and installation
  • Panel wiring, addressing and loop configuration
Fire & Gas and Security in detail

Piping prefabrication and installation, supports, mechanical valves and QA/QC inspection services.

  • Prefabrication of pipe spools in shop conditions
  • Installation of process, utility and instrument piping
  • Installation of static equipment — vessels, exchangers, columns
  • Rotating equipment installation and alignment support
Mechanical & Piping in detail

Hot and cold service insulation for process piping, vessels and equipment, with fabrication and cladding.

  • Process piping, steam lines and headers
  • Vessels, columns, exchangers and boilers
  • Chilled water, refrigerant and cryogenic line insulation
  • Vapour barrier detailing to prevent moisture ingress
Thermal Insulation in detail

Cable trenches, duct banks, equipment plinths and plant roadworks supporting electrical and mechanical scopes.

  • Cable trench excavation, construction and reinstatement
  • Duct bank construction and encasement
  • Equipment and panel plinth construction
  • Pump, skid and package foundation works
General Civil Works in detail

Turnaround, shutdown, planned and breakdown maintenance under long-term contracts and call-out agreements.

  • Shutdown scope development and pre-fabrication planning
  • Multi-discipline execution teams mobilised to schedule
  • Maintenance planning and scheduling support
  • Predictive and preventive maintenance programmes
